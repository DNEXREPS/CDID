== CDID Trans ==
The CDID Trans is a '''building''' used for Travel job, but it can also be used to teleport to several places, but you have to pay Rp. 1,000,000 if you want to teleport. Located in almost all maps/cities[[File:Cdidtravel.png.png|thumb|CDID Trans]]
