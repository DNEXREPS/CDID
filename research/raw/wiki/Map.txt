The map is an essential component of open world video games, providing players with a visual representation of the game world and allowing them to navigate and explore their surroundings. In the context of open world video games set in Indonesia, the map plays a crucial role in immersing players in the rich and diverse virtual environment inspired by real-life locations in the country.

Open world video games set in Indonesia often feature expansive and detailed maps that showcase the country's diverse landscapes, cultural landmarks, and architectural wonders. These maps are meticulously crafted to recreate the essence of Indonesia, including its lush rainforests, stunning beaches, towering mountains, bustling cities, and traditional villages.

== Jakarta Map==
===Jakarta===
[[File:RobloxScreenShot20240129 193828769.png|thumb|225x225px|Jakarta in CDID]]
Jakarta, the bustling capital of Indonesia, pulsates with diverse experiences for every traveler. Officially named the Special Capital Region of Jakarta, it sprawls across the northwest coast of Java, boasting the title of Southeast Asia's largest city. Beyond its metropolitan charm, Jakarta is also the diplomatic heart of ASEAN, fostering regional unity.

Embrace the city's iconic landmarks by embarking on a car tour. Witness the majestic National Monument (Monas) towering over Merdeka Square, the historical significance of Bundaran HI, and the electrifying atmosphere of Gelora Bung Karno Stadium (GBK).

But venture beyond the beaten path and delve into South Jakarta's hidden gems. Immerse yourself in the vibrant neighborhoods of Melawai and Blok M, brimming with eclectic shops, delectable restaurants, and buzzing entertainment venues. These adjoining areas offer a unique glimpse into the city's soul, catering to every taste and style.

For car enthusiasts, Jakarta caters to your passion too. Renowned dealerships like Tokoma/Toyota, Liaxe/Lexus, Mestibis/Mitsubishi, BMR/BMW, Murceso-Venz/Mercedes-Benz, Hyunson/Hyundai, and Wuliuz/Wuling line the streets, showcasing the latest automotive marvels. Jakarta also offer Modification for player cars for who want deeper into personalized car havens.

Whether you seek historical grandeur, cultural immersion, or modern delights, Jakarta has something for you. So, pack your bags, explore its diverse offerings, and discover the true essence of this captivating Indonesian metropolis.

===Bekasi===
Bekasi City, a vibrant and growing metropolis in Indonesia, offers a variety of attractions for visitors of all interests.

One of the most popular tourist destinations in Bekasi is the Summarecon Bekasi shopping mall, which features a wide range of shops, restaurants, and entertainment options. The mall is also home to the Upside Down Pyramid, a gravity-defying attraction that is sure to leave visitors feeling dizzy and disoriented.

For automotive enthusiasts, Bekasi is a great place to visit the Toyota/Tokoma Factory, where visitors can learn about the manufacturing process of Toyota cars and test drive the latest models. The city also has a drag race track and a drift spot where visitors can experience the thrill of high-speed racing.

In addition to its shopping and automotive attractions, Bekasi also has a number of educational and cultural institutions. The SMA School CDID is a prestigious private school that offers a rigorous academic program. The Graha Hanfarhan housing complex is home to a number of museums and art galleries.

Bekasi is also a major commercial center, with a number of car dealerships located throughout the city. ===BSD===

If you're interested in getting your adrenaline pumping, you can head to the Edutown Drift Arena, where you can learn how to drift your car or watch others do it. There is also an offroad track in Pagedangan where you can take your off-road vehicle for a spin.

For those looking for a place to live, BSD City offers a variety of housing options, from apartments and condominiums to houses and townhouses. ===Bandung===

Bandung, the capital of West Java, is a vibrant and cosmopolitan city with a rich history and culture. It is home to a diverse range of attractions, from iconic landmarks to hidden gems.

One of Bandung's most iconic landmarks is the Gedung Sate, a Neo-Renaissance-style building that houses the seat of the provincial government. The building is known for its distinctive onion-shaped dome and its four giant bronze statues of buffaloes. Another popular tourist destination is the Jembatan Pasupati, a cable-stayed bridge that connects the city's north and south sides. The bridge offers stunning views of the city and the surrounding mountains. For a taste of local culture, head to Dago, a trendy neighborhood that is home to a variety of restaurants, cafes, and shops. Dago is also a great place to people-watch and enjoy the city's vibrant atmosphere. If you are looking for a more spiritual experience, visit the Pusdai, a large mosque that is located in the heart of the city. The mosque is a popular destination for Muslims from all over the world. Finally, no visit to Bandung would be complete without a trip to Pasteur, a bustling street that is home to a variety of shops, restaurants, and nightlife options. Pasteur is a great place to find everything from souvenirs to traditional Indonesian cuisine.

In addition to these iconic landmarks, Bandung is also home to a number of dealerships that sell a variety of cars. Some of the most popular dealerships include: Sumberjaya Bandung, Honade, Sanziky, Otnas Espirit Works. These dealerships offer a wide range of services, including sales, service, and parts. They also have a variety of financing options available to make it easier for customers to purchase a new car.

Bandung is another hub for car enthusiasts in Indonesia, boasting a thriving car modification scene with numerous workshops and events.From classic restorations to cutting-edge performance upgrades, Bandung's car modification scene caters to diverse styles and budgets.

Bandung is a great place to visit for a weekend getaway or a longer vacation. With its rich history, culture, and vibrant atmosphere, Bandung has something to offer everyone.
