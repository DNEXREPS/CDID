== CDID Wiki ==
'''Car Driving Indonesia''' (or '''CDID''') is a town-and-city driving/roleplaying game based in Indonesia, created by user [https://www.roblox.com/users/2584360064/profile/ Pengemudiindonesiaa], otherwise known as 'shad'. As of August 2025, CDID has had over '''336M+''' visits.

'''Car Driving Indonesia''' (atau '''CDID''') adalah sebuah game kendaraan Indonesia yang berbasis di Indonesia. Dibuat oleh [https://www.roblox.com/users/2584360064/profile/ Pengemudiindonesiaa] atau biasa disebut shad. Sampai Agustus 2025, CDID memiliki pengunjung sebanyak '''336 Juta+'''.
 '''<big>Current Game Version: V.2.2.0</big>'''
