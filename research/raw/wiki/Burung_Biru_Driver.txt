'''Burung Biru''' (English: '''BlueBird''') is a job featured in [[CDID Wiki|CDID]]. The player assumes the role of a taxi driver and must drive passengers to destinations marked all across the map.

== Guide ==
* First, view '''Job Selection''' at the spawn point of the map you're in and select '''Burung Biru Driver'''. Your character will appear dressed in a taxi driver outfit.
* Then, drive to the spawn point. location will depend on what map you're in (see '''Spawn Points''' below).
* Upon arriving at the spawn point, initiate the job through '''Start Job Here'''. A waypoint will be set, as well as a blue arrow above your character wll appear, pointing directly towards a passenger's pick-up point.
* Spawn a taxi from one of the spawn bays in the designated area.
* Drive the taxi over to the waypoint.
* Once you reach the waypoint, pull up beside the passenger to pick them up - once you pick them up, drive over to their specified location.
* After you successfully drop off a passenger, you will be directed to your next passenger's pick-up point and so on.


== Spawn Points ==
There are currently two spawn points featured in [[CDID Wiki|CDID]]. Note that, despite the '''Burung Biru Driver''' job being avaliable in the '''Job Selection''' menu in the [[Central Java]] map, there is no specific location to start the job at.

== Trivia ==
* '''Burung Biru''' literally translates to "Blue Bird" in English; Blue Bird (or Blue Bird Group) is an Indonesian transportation company based in Jakarta, and is one of the largest and most popular taxi service companies in the country, offering a wide variety of services and fleet vehicles.
** The transportation company '''TrueBird''' featured on the side of the taxi is a parody of Blue Bird.
* Despite the '''Burung Biru''' job being avaliable in the '''Job Selection''' menu in the [[Central Java]] map, there is no specific location to start the job at. The waypoint simply points towards an inaccessible location outside the map, and is therefore either a glitch or a developer oversight.
* The '''Burung Biru''' Taxi features the same license plate (B 1740 OVQ) as the '''Tokoma Hiace Ambulans''' featured in the [[Emergency Service]] job.
