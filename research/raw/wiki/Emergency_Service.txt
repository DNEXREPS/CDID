'''Emergency Service''' (Indonesian: '''Layanan darurat''') is a job featured in [[CDID Wiki|CDID]]. The player assumes the role of an ambulance driver (paramedic) and must drive to patients all across the map and tend to their injuries with medkits.

== Guide ==
* First, view '''Job Selection''' at the spawn point of the map you're in and select '''Emergency Service'''. Your character will appear dressed in a paramedic outfit.
* Then, drive to the hospital. The hospital's location will depend on what map you're in (see '''Hospitals''' below).
* Upon arriving at the hospital, initiate the job through '''Start Job Here'''. A waypoint will be set, as well as a blue arrow above your character will appear, pointing directly towards it.
* Spawn an ambulance from one of the spawn bays in the hospital's parking lot.
** There are four ambulances to choose from, and have a random chance upon being spawned (see '''Ambulances''' below).
* Drive the ambulance over to the waypoint. You may turn on the emergency lights and siren to alert other drivers as you make your way there through the ambulance's control panel in the right of the screen.
* Once you reach the waypoint, pull up beside the patient and pick up a '''medkit''' from the rear of your ambulance by pressing '''E'''.
* Walk over to the patient and "heal" them using the '''medkit''', once again by pressing '''E'''. If successful, the patient will stand upright and walk away.

== Trivia ==
* Cara Hospitals in BSD has a '''UGD'''; '''UGD''' stands for "''Unit Gawat Darurat''", which is Indonesian for "''emergency department''" (literally translates to "''urgent emergency unit''").
* The '''Tokoma Hiace Ambulans''' features the same license plate (B 1740 OVQ) as the Tokoma Vios Taxi featured in the [[Burung Biru Driver]] job.
